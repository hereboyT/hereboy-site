Privacy policy

Last updated 8 November 2026

Who we are

This website, hereboy.co.za, is operated by Hereboy Studios, a mobile game studio based in Cape Town, South Africa. Questions about this policy can be sent to tman@hereboy.co.za.

What this website collects

This website has no accounts, forms, advertising or analytics, and it does not set cookies.

Like most websites, it is delivered by a hosting provider, which may record standard technical logs such as IP addresses and the pages requested, in order to run and secure the service. We do not use those logs to identify visitors.

If you email us

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you include in your message. We use this only to reply to you and to keep a record of the conversation.

Our games

Our games are in development and are not yet available. Before each game is released, we will publish a privacy policy for that game here, describing any data it collects, including any collected by advertising or payment services it uses.

Your rights

Under South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA), you may ask to see the personal information we hold about you, and ask us to correct or delete it. To make a request, email us at the address above. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator of South Africa.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
